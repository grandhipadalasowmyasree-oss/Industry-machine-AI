INDUSTRIAL MACHINE MAINTENANCE GUIDE

Document ID: MG-001
Version: 1.0

1. GENERAL MAINTENANCE

Regular maintenance is required to keep industrial machines operating safely and efficiently. Maintenance should be performed according to the machine operating schedule and approved procedures.

2. MOTOR OVERHEATING

If the motor temperature becomes unusually high, the following conditions should be inspected:

- Cooling system condition
- Ventilation around the motor
- Motor load
- Bearing condition
- Lubrication condition
- Operating environment

The machine should not be operated outside its specified operating limits.

3. HIGH VIBRATION

High vibration may indicate a mechanical or operating problem. The following should be inspected:

- Bearing condition
- Shaft alignment
- Loose components
- Mechanical wear
- Motor mounting
- Operating load

If abnormal vibration continues, the machine should be inspected according to the approved maintenance procedure.

4. BEARING MAINTENANCE

Bearings should be inspected regularly for abnormal temperature, vibration, noise, wear, and lubrication problems.

If bearing temperature increases significantly, inspect lubrication, alignment, bearing condition, and cooling conditions.

5. PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE

Preventive maintenance should include:

- Regular inspection of machine components
- Checking temperature and vibration
- Checking oil and lubrication levels
- Inspecting bearings
- Checking electrical connections
- Recording maintenance activities

6. MAINTENANCE RECORDS

All maintenance activities should be recorded with the date, machine identification, observed condition, action performed, and responsible personnel.
