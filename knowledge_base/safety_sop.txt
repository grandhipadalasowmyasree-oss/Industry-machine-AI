INDUSTRIAL MACHINE SAFETY STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

Document ID: SOP-001
Version: 1.0

1. PURPOSE

This procedure provides general safety guidance for operating, inspecting, and maintaining industrial machinery.

2. PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT

Personnel working near industrial machinery should use the required personal protective equipment based on the site risk assessment. Typical PPE may include:

- Safety helmet
- Safety glasses
- Safety footwear
- Protective gloves when appropriate
- Hearing protection in high-noise areas
- Protective clothing when required

3. BEFORE OPERATING A MACHINE

Before starting a machine, the operator should:

- Check the machine for visible damage.
- Verify that guards and protective devices are in place.
- Check for unusual leaks.
- Check that the operating area is clear.
- Confirm that emergency stop devices are accessible.
- Verify that required inspections have been completed.
- Review any active warnings or alarms.

4. ABNORMAL MACHINE CONDITIONS

If a machine shows abnormal temperature, vibration, noise, pressure, or other warning signs:

- Stop and assess the situation according to the approved site procedure.
- Do not bypass safety devices.
- Keep unauthorized personnel away from the affected area.
- Report the abnormal condition to the responsible maintenance personnel.
- Follow the applicable troubleshooting and maintenance procedure.

5. MACHINE MAINTENANCE SAFETY

Before performing maintenance:

- Follow the site's approved isolation procedure.
- Shut down the machine using the appropriate procedure.
- Isolate relevant energy sources.
- Verify that the machine cannot unexpectedly start.
- Use appropriate PPE.
- Use approved tools and equipment.
- Follow the machine-specific maintenance instructions.

6. EMERGENCY PROCEDURE

In an emergency:

- Follow the site's emergency response procedure.
- Use the emergency stop when appropriate.
- Move personnel away from hazardous areas.
- Notify the responsible supervisor or emergency response team.
- Do not restart equipment until it has been inspected and cleared according to the approved procedure.

7. PROHIBITED ACTIONS

Personnel should not:

- Bypass safety guards.
- Disable safety interlocks.
- Operate machinery outside approved limits.
- Perform maintenance without proper authorization.
- Ignore repeated alarms or abnormal machine conditions.
- Restart equipment before required safety checks are completed.

8. REPORTING

All significant machine abnormalities, safety incidents, and maintenance activities should be documented according to site procedures.
