INDUSTRIAL MACHINE TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

Document ID: TG-001
Version: 1.0

1. PURPOSE

This guide provides general troubleshooting information for common abnormal conditions observed in industrial machines.

2. HIGH TEMPERATURE

Possible causes:

- Excessive machine load
- Poor ventilation
- Cooling system problems
- Bearing problems
- Insufficient lubrication
- Extended operation

Recommended checks:

- Check the machine load.
- Inspect the cooling system.
- Check ventilation.
- Inspect bearing condition.
- Check lubrication.
- Review recent maintenance records.

3. HIGH VIBRATION

Possible causes:

- Shaft misalignment
- Bearing wear
- Loose components
- Mechanical imbalance
- Excessive operating load
- Mechanical damage

Recommended checks:

- Inspect bearings.
- Check shaft alignment.
- Inspect mounting and fasteners.
- Check for mechanical imbalance.
- Review the operating load.
- Compare current vibration with normal operating conditions.

4. LOW OIL PRESSURE

Possible causes:

- Low oil level
- Oil leakage
- Blocked filter
- Pump problem
- Incorrect oil condition

Recommended checks:

- Check oil level.
- Inspect for visible leaks.
- Inspect the oil filter.
- Check the lubrication system.
- Follow the machine-specific maintenance procedure.

5. ABNORMAL NOISE

Possible causes:

- Bearing wear
- Loose components
- Mechanical friction
- Misalignment
- Damaged components

Recommended checks:

- Identify the source of the noise.
- Inspect bearings.
- Check fasteners and mounting.
- Inspect alignment.
- Follow the approved maintenance procedure.

6. MACHINE NOT OPERATING

Possible causes:

- Electrical supply problem
- Safety interlock active
- Control system fault
- Emergency stop activated
- Mechanical fault

Recommended checks:

- Check the machine status and alarms.
- Verify the emergency stop status.
- Check the electrical supply according to the approved procedure.
- Check safety interlocks.
- Contact authorized maintenance personnel when required.

7. REPEATED WARNINGS

If the same warning occurs repeatedly:

- Record the warning information.
- Review recent machine conditions.
- Check maintenance records.
- Do not repeatedly reset or bypass safety warnings.
- Escalate the issue to authorized maintenance personnel.

8. IMPORTANT SAFETY NOTE

Troubleshooting information is general guidance. Always follow the machine manufacturer's instructions and the organization's approved safety procedures.
